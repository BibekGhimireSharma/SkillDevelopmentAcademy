The Person Behind It All

Meet Bishal Poudel

"I didn't start this academy because I had all the resources — I started it because I saw too many talented young people in Butwal struggling to find practical skills that the job market actually demands. Knowledge without application is just theory. I wanted to change that."

Bishal Poudel is an experienced accountant and educator based in Butwal, Rupandehi. Having spent years navigating the real-world demands of finance, taxation, and business operations in Nepal, he recognized a critical gap: quality, practical, affordable skills training was nearly impossible to find locally.

Driven by a mission to empower youth — regardless of background or resources — Bishal founded Skill Development Academy in Devinagar, Chaupari, Butwal-11. His philosophy is simple: every student deserves one-on-one computer access, an experienced mentor, and a clear path to employment.

Today, the academy serves students from SEE level through to working professionals, offering everything from basic computing to advanced VAT filing and accounting software — all with personal attention, certified outcomes, and genuine care for each student's future.
